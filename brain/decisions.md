## Product and business rules

- BillZen's primary job is company money received/money paid, receipts/payment vouchers and understandable balances. Billing, inventory and CRM extend this foundation.
- Operate fully offline without a mandatory account, cloud, paid API or network. Initially one owner, independent local records on each device. Shared staff/device synchronization is deferred until transfers and billing are complete; no sync provider selected.
- Focus on India and GCC. Preserve English/Malayalam/Arabic, RTL, business timezone, integer minor units and quantities scaled by1000. Currency formatting does not establish statutory tax/e-invoicing compliance.
- Dashboard opens Today's receipts/payments, with simple and analytical views and day/month/year/custom filtering. Keep business language understandable to a nontechnical owner.
- Negative cash balance: warn, then allow only after explicit confirmation. Re-read the live balance when posting.
- Customer cash history is cash movement, not invoice debt. Account transfers are balance movements, not business revenue/expense. Cash movement is not profit.
- Stock changes need additive immutable history. Add/Reduce requires a reason; corrections reverse a movement instead of editing it away. Stock adjustment alone must not create a cash payment.
- Draft invoices must not affect stock, money, debt or final numbering. Finalization/settlement/supplier purchases are separate phases.
